## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Umrah & Hajj Companion (رفيق العمرة والحج) guides pilgrims through Umrah and Hajj, step by step, in English, Arabic and French.

UMRAH GUIDE
Ihram, Talbiyah, entering the Haram, Tawaf, Maqam Ibrahim, Sa'i and Taqsir: every step is explained simply, with its rules and practical tips.

HAJJ GUIDE
Hajj day by day, from 8 to 13 Dhul Hijjah: types of Hajj (Tamattu', Qiran, Ifrad), Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, stoning the Jamarat, sacrifice, shaving, Tawaf al-Ifadah, the days of Tashriq and the farewell Tawaf.

THE DU'AS OF EVERY STEP
For each moment of the rites: the vocalised Arabic text, transliteration, translation and number of repetitions. Listen to the available recordings, even offline.

LAP COUNTER
Count your Tawaf and Sa'i laps with a single tap, with a vibration at each lap and the du'a of the moment on screen.

PRAYER TIMES AND REMINDERS
Prayer times where you are, the next prayer, and a reminder at each prayer time or a few minutes before.

QIBLA AND MIQAT
The Qibla direction using your phone's compass, and the nearest miqat based on your location.

HOLY SITES
Interactive map of the sites of Makkah and Madinah, with directions to each one and emergency numbers.

ALSO
• Travel checklist so you forget nothing (documents, ihram, health, Hajj)
• Collection of du'as for every situation
• Practical messages published during the season
• Dark mode and adjustable text size
• Works offline for the essentials

Free app. It shows discreet banner ads, never during the rites (guide, counter, du'as, Qibla).

This app is a companion tool: for any religious question, consult trusted scholars.
```
